How it works

From farm to buyer in three steps

  1. 1

    List or search

    Sellers post produce with price and quantity. Buyers search by crop, place and price.

  2. 2

    Compare and connect

    Move it to market

    Find a transporter on your route, weigh and inspect, then pay safely.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Mkulima Market free to use?

Yes. Buyers always use it free, and sellers can list up to 5 items free.

How do I get paid?

You agree payment directly with the buyer, usually M-Pesa. Weigh and inspect produce first, and keep your M-Pesa messages as records.

Can I use it in Kiswahili?

Yes. Switch language at the top of any page, and ask Mkulima AI in Kiswahili.

Is my location shared?

Only an approximate area (about 1 km) is shown to others, never your exact farm or home.

Does it work without internet?

Install the app from your browser. Pages you have opened keep working offline, and new data loads when you reconnect.
