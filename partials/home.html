    Questions, answered

    Everything about selling, transport and your account.

    Still have a question?Ask Mkulima AI
    Who can join Mkulima Market?

    Sellers (farmers, fishers, herders and traders) and transporters can create an account. Anyone can browse listings and ask Mkulima AI without an account.

    Is Mkulima Market free to use?

    Yes. Browsing and asking Mkulima AI are free. Sellers can keep up to 5 listings live for free, with 5 photos each.

    How do I get paid?

    You agree payment directly with the buyer, usually M-Pesa. Weigh and inspect produce first, and keep your M-Pesa messages as records.

    How do transporters find loads?

    List your route, vehicle and capacity. Sellers along your route see you on the map, and Mkulima AI suggests you when someone needs transport.

    What is the blue badge?

    A one-time KSh 2,000 verification. Our team visits your farm, business or vehicle, and verified profiles show a blue badge. Paying alone never earns it.

    Can I use it in Kiswahili?

    Yes. Switch language at the top of any page, and ask Mkulima AI in Kiswahili.

    Is my location shared?

    Only an approximate area (about 1 km) is shown to others, never your exact farm or home.

    Does it work without internet?

    Install the app from your browser. Pages you have opened keep working offline, and new data loads when you reconnect.
